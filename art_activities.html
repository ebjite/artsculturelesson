Grammatical constructs

Passive measurement + medium
"…a monumental oil painting — 3.49 × 7.76m — rendered in…"
Present-tense list of subjects
"A screaming horse dominates… a dismembered soldier lies…"
Semicolon + metaphor of equivalence
"…grey is the colour of ash, of newsprint, of moral ambiguity"
Verb + comparative clause ('mirrors')
"…create disorientation that mirrors the violence of…"
Theorist citation + present-tense application
"Through trauma theory (Judith Herman, 1992), the painting enacts…"
Paradox / contrast as closing claim
"Guernica does not show war; it performs…"

MESSAGE
WHAT
Passive measurement + medium — Establishes objective physical facts. Passive voice removes the writer — no feelings yet.
WHAT
Present-tense list of subjects — Describes composition with active verbs. Lists what the eye sees — no interpretation.
HOW
Semicolon + metaphor — Semicolon extends an argument. Metaphor shows interpretation of technique — feeling, not fact.
HOW
Verb + comparative clause — 'Create' + 'mirrors' links technique to psychological effect. That link = HOW.
MSG
Theorist citation — Named in brackets. Applied with strong verb: 'enacts' vs 'represents' — precision matters.
MSG
Paradox as closing claim — Two contrasting verbs (show / perform) = the analytical claim in miniature. Classic MESSAGE move.

WHAT
C — Vitruvian Man is a pen-and-ink drawing on paper, ~34 × 24cm, depicting a male figure in two superimposed positions inscribed within both a circle and a square.
WHAT
F — Handwritten notes in mirror script surround the figure on all sides, filling every margin with measurement and observation.

D — The double image creates an uncanny oscillation: the figure seems to breathe, to refuse to be fixed — ideal and reality held in tension.

Which sentence does the most analytical work — and why?
Which one couldn't exist without the others? Which makes the biggest intellectual leap?
★ The gold standard sentence

Sentence D — "Banksy literalises Debord's argument" — is the strongest. It doesn't just name the theory — it shows the artwork enacting the theory in real time. The verb 'literalises' argues that the act of destruction IS the theoretical argument made visible. That is the difference between applying a theory and embodying one.
