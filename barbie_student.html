HUMN 110 Arts and Culture  |  Oral Exam  |  2-Minute Format

Barbie (2023)

50 Questions  ·  American University of Phnom Penh
How the oral exam works: A spinning wheel will select you at random. Your teacher will ask you one question. You have 2 minutes to answer. Your teacher may then ask the follow-up shown in italics. Give reasons. Connect to class theories where possible. There is no single correct answer. The quality of your thinking matters most. References are provided for students who want to read further.
50 questions

Follow-up: Can something be made by a huge company like Mattel and still be art? What does that question say about how we define art?
Further ReadingBerger, J. (1972). Ways of Seeing. Penguin. / Dissanayake, E. (1988). What Is Art For? University of Washington Press.
